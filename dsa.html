Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)

Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) are fundamental concepts in computer science that involve the organization of data and the methods for processing that data efficiently.

Understanding DSA is crucial for solving complex problems, optimizing performance, and improving the efficiency of software applications.

Key topics include arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, sorting algorithms, and searching algorithms.

Mastering DSA enhances problem-solving skills and is essential for technical interviews and competitive programming.
